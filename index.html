About

Rudo Menge is a graphic designer and art director based in Groningen, the Netherlands. For more than twenty-five years the studio has made posters, identities, books and exhibitions for museums, theatres, orchestras and luxury brands.

The work grows out of long collaborations: two decades of exhibition campaigns and the magazine for the Groninger Museum, seasons for Noord Nederlands Toneel and Noord Nederlands Orkest, books with Studio Job, and print for Cartier.
