About

Rudo Menge is a graphic designer and art director in Groningen. With more than twenty-five years of experience in campaigns, book designs, graphic identities and exhibition design, the studio is open to new projects.

Selected projects include exhibition campaigns and the magazine for the Groninger Museum, campaigns for Noord-Nederlands Toneel and Noord-Nederlands Orkest, book designs for Studio Job and Iris van Herpen, and print for Cartier.
